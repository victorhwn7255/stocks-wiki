# NOW — ServiceNow, Inc.

## Thesis role

ServiceNow is the vault's **first pure application-layer / SaaS company page** — an enterprise software platform (IT/HR/security/CRM workflows) that is a *consumer* of AI compute, not a supplier of any datacenter-supply-chain component. It is included narrowly, for the [[software-AI-moat-durability]] research goal: NOW is the canonical **system-of-record + workflow-lock-in** archetype, and the primary-source test of whether a seat-billed incumbent can survive the AI transition. Per honest-verdict discipline (Section 2.1), its fit to the broader supply-chain thesis is weak by design — this page is verdict-oriented around the research goal rather than supply-chain centrality.

**Placement framing.** NOW carries `layer: outside` + `outside` on all five per-domain tiers, following the demand-side placement precedent set by [[MSFT]]. NOW sits even further off the supply-side value-capture ladder: an application-layer SaaS that merely *rents* the infrastructure the vault tracks downstream. It captures no supply-side chokepoint rent. **Demand-side placement CODIFIED (S189):** CLAUDE.md §3.21 + frameworks.md Framework 10.1 — NOW = the **app-layer-consumer** sub-role; `_thesis.md` unchanged.

**Research-goal verdict (Q2 2026 refresh):** **CONTESTED, leaning durable — HELD, and the durable side STRENGTHENED.** The AI monetization the Q1 verdict was waiting on materialized: **ServiceNow AI ACV crossed $1B, accelerating** (see below). But the **contested side is unchanged** — AI is still call-only bookings (no clean Tier-1 revenue line) and organic-vs-inorganic AI attribution is still not broken out. The verdict strengthens on materialization; it is not resolved.

## Billed-unit lens & AI monetization

This is the page's reason for inclusion — the [[software-AI-moat-durability]] **billed-unit discriminator** (seat / consumption / outcome). NOW is the canonical **seat-billed incumbent actively converting to consumption**, and Q2 is the quarter the AI monetization *materialized*:

- **★ AI ACV crossed $1B, accelerating.** "ServiceNow AI continued to outperform expectations in Q2, with ACV crossing over $1 billion and net-new ACV growth accelerating sequentially, growing over 40% quarter-over-quarter" (CFO Mastantuono, Q2 2026 call); on track to beat the **$1.5B end-2026 ACV target** (McDermott). Deals with 5+ AI products grew **5.5× YoY** (driving a *tripling* of $1M+ AI deals); customers with **agentic AI in production grew 9× over 9 months**; first-time agentic buyers **+45% YoY**. This is the materialization the Q1 verdict (">$600M tracking $1B") was waiting for.
- **The seat→consumption conversion held and is priced.** "50%, five zero, of our net-new business is already non-seat based" (McDermott — held from Q1); a **hybrid model** (license + usage); **AI-native SKU pricing uplift 20–30%**, ProPlus **>30%** (Mastantuono). Seat-deflation evidence sharpened: L1 autonomous agents now close **80–85% of service requests** without human intervention (Zavery) — a capability metric, still not a portfolio-aggregate seat-reduction figure.
- **★ But the contested side is unchanged (the disclosure-quality gap persists).** AI ACV is **still call-only bookings — no clean Tier-1 AI revenue line** in the 10-Q (cRPO/RPO/subscription are disclosed, AI is not broken out); and **organic-vs-inorganic AI attribution is still not disclosed** — McDermott again deflected the Q1 skeptics' question (reframing to "a C-suite story"). The verdict strengthens on materialization but the same two disclosure gaps the Q1 verdict flagged remain open (Source audit notes; Open questions #1 + #3).

## Durability & the system-of-record moat

- **Retention/backlog:** 98% renewal; cRPO $13.2B (+21.5% cc) / RPO $29.0B; subscription gross margin guide 81%; **658 customers >$5M ACV** (+32 crossing $20M YoY); 123 deals >$1M NNACV (+40% YoY); 18 of top-20 deals were ITSM, 12 Core Business Workflows. As customers scale they "consolidate more on the ServiceNow AI Platform" (the lock-in thesis).
- **The moat argument (system-of-record archetype):** "AI Control Tower" (500+ customers live in 6 months; a kill-switch for rogue agents) + "Context Engine" — the claim that agents need NOW's proprietary, governed workflow context (100B+ workflows, ~8T transactions on the platform). The durable-leg thesis; still a claim, not a disclosed revenue moat.
- **The contested side (the persistent watch item):** organic-vs-inorganic AI uplift is still not broken out (the Q1 skepticism — Weiss/Borges — deflected again). NOW's AI build-out remains heavily inorganic (EmployeeWorks/Moveworks, Veza, Armis, Pyramid) — EmployeeWorks +150% QoQ deal volume, but no organic-AI revision figure. This is the unresolved half of the verdict.

## Open questions

*(§3.19b lifecycle applied.)*

1. **Clean AI revenue line.** Will NOW disclose AI *revenue* (Tier 1), or does AI ACV stay a call-only bookings construct? Unchanged at Q2 (still call-only). A clean line would strengthen the durable verdict.
2. **Seat deflation at portfolio scale.** L1 agents now auto-close 80–85% of requests — does that generalize into a measurable fulfiller-seat reduction, and does the consumption overlay (50% non-seat) offset it? Sharpened, not resolved.
3. **★ Organic vs inorganic AI uplift — the key unresolved item.** Deflected again at Q2. Can NOW show organic AI revisions, or does AI growth stay attributable to Moveworks/Veza/Armis? This is the central contested-leg test.
4. **Core-module cannibalization.** Partially addressed — McDermott: "not getting negotiated down on the core"; 50% non-seat. Watch whether the seat-priced core compresses as AI-native SKUs scale.
5. **Placement codification — RESOLVED (S189).** CLAUDE.md §3.21 + frameworks.md Framework 10.1 (app-layer-consumer).
