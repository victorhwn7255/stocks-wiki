# GOOGL — Alphabet Inc.

## Thesis role

Alphabet is the vault's **second hyperscaler / demand-side page** ([[MSFT]], S112, was the first) and the flagship ingest of the hyperscaler **capex-payer** program — the companies that *fund* the AI-datacenter buildout the rest of the vault tracks downstream (program scope: [[MSFT]] + GOOGL + [[AMZN]] + [[META]] + [[ORCL]] + the rent-side [[CRWV]]/[[NBIS]]). It is ingested for two purposes: (1) as a **direct primary-source hyperscaler-CAPEX signal** feeding [[AI-demand-durability]] + the [[hyperscaler-capex]] tracker — Alphabet is one of the four hyperscalers underlying the ~$640-720B 2026 aggregate the chokepoint thesis depends on; and (2) as a **custom-silicon operator** (TPU + Axion) for [[hyperscaler-custom-ASIC]] and [[AI-agentic-CPU-orchestration-reemergence]]. It is *not* a supply-chain value-capture participant, and — unlike [[MSFT]]/[[NOW]]/[[PLTR]] — it is *not* on the [[software-AI-moat-durability]] application-layer scorecard (it is a hyperscaler/platform, not a seat/consumption/outcome SaaS name).

**Placement framing (honest-verdict, Section 2.1).** A hyperscaler is the **demand source** (Framework 3 dependency map) and the **CAPEX deployer** (Framework 10 allocation starting point), not a position within the six supply-side value-capture layers. Alphabet therefore carries `layer: outside` + `outside` on all five per-domain tiers — it captures no supply-side chokepoint rent; it *funds* the chokepoints.

**Demand-side placement — CODIFIED (S189, 2026-07-19).** GOOGL was one of the four demand-side pages (MSFT S112 → NOW S113 → PLTR S114 → DDOG S188) that flagged the frameworks app-layer-gap; the placement is now codified — **CLAUDE.md Section 3.21 + frameworks.md Framework 10.1**. `layer: outside` + all-`*_tier: outside` is a **documented value** for the **capex-payer** sub-role (the *source* of the Framework 10 CAPEX flow — the hyperscaler-as-custom-silicon-commissioner role, where Alphabet designs TPUs and [[AVGO]] builds them, sits within it). ## CAPEX & demand-durability

This is the section the hyperscaler-payer program exists to source — Alphabet's capex is a primary-source demand signal for the chokepoint thesis (cross-ref [[AI-demand-durability]] + the [[hyperscaler-capex]] payer-comparison tracker).

- **★ FY2026 capex guidance RAISED to $195–205B** (from $180–190B). CFO Ashkenazi (verbatim): *"updating our full-year 2026 CapEx guidance range to $195 billion–$205 billion, up from our previous estimate of $180 billion–$190 billion. The increase in the range is primarily due to an acceleration in the delivery of capacity to meet growing demand."* **This is a cleaner demand signal than the Q1 raise** — Q1's increase was attributed to the Intersect Power *energy* acquisition; this one is **pure demand-pull capacity acceleration.**
- **Q2 2026 capex $44.9B** (up from $35.7B in Q1); **~60% servers / ~40% data centers + networking** (Ashkenazi). Trailing-12-month capex ~$185.7B.
- **2027 capex guided to "increase significantly" vs 2026** (Ashkenazi; details later) — after roughly doubling in 2026 (~$91B → ~$200B), Alphabet is set to step up again.
- **Energy as a named forward cost.** Ashkenazi: scaling technical infrastructure "will continue to put pressure on the P&L in the form of higher depreciation expense and related data center operations costs, such as **energy**" — a direct primary-source link from hyperscaler capex to the power/energy chokepoint (the vault's binding-2026-constraint thesis).
- **★ Backlog $514B** (Google Cloud RPO), up **>$50B sequentially** from $462.3B; >50% expected to recognize as revenue over the next 24 months (~$257B runway). The demand-side mirror of the supply-side backlogs the rest of the vault reports.

The ~$200B Alphabet figure primary-sources a second (now larger) hyperscaler component of the ~$640-720B 2026 aggregate underlying [[AI-demand-durability]] (alongside [[MSFT]]'s ~$190B). Framework 10 (CAPEX flow) treats Alphabet as a top-of-diagram demand source.

- **Q2 2026 free cash flow −$5.9B** (capex-driven; trailing-12-month FCF $53.3B) — the capex now runs ahead of quarterly operating cash flow.
- **Honest-verdict (Section 2.1):** the FCF-below-capex + debt-expansion dynamic is *present* but **far milder than [[ORCL]] / [[CRWV]]** — Alphabet carries a fortress balance sheet (~$242.5B cash + marketable securities), so the financing "risk" is a funding-mix observation, not a solvency question. ## Google Cloud inflection

- **Revenue $24.8B in Q2 2026, +82% YoY** — the acceleration continues (from +63% in Q1).
- **Operating income $8.8B** (more than tripled YoY), margin **35.6%** (from 20.7% — +1,490 bps) — revenue acceleration *and* margin expansion simultaneously.
- **Supply-constrained.** Ashkenazi: *"we're still in a supply-constrained environment... we've said this now for multiple quarters in a row, we are seeing very strong demand."* Alphabet plans to **expand third-party capacity in Q3 as a bridging strategy** while building internal capacity — a near-term margin-mix headwind, and a demand read (it is renting because it is sold out).
- **Enterprise AI as the growth driver:** Gemini Enterprise adoption ~90% of Fortune 100 (Pichai).

## Custom silicon — TPU + Axion (the interconnection to the compute chokepoint)

Alphabet operates the vault's most mature hyperscaler custom-silicon program — and Q2 marked a **milestone**:

- **★ External TPU sales now recognizing revenue.** Alphabet *"began to recognize revenues from TPU system sales, which we delivered to customer data centers for the first time in Q2"* (Ashkenazi). This moves TPU from "planned/beginning" (the Q1 framing) to **delivering + recognizing** — captive silicon becoming a merchant-ish hardware offering. Revenue is small in 2026 (inventory-building phase) with the **vast majority realized in 2027**, lumpy by shipment timing; the agreements flow into the $514B Cloud backlog.
- **8th-generation TPUs** — TPU 8t (training) + TPU 8i (inference); **Axion** (Arm-based server CPU) is the host CPU for both, replacing x86 hosts. Cross-ref [[hyperscaler-custom-ASIC]] + [[AI-agentic-CPU-orchestration-reemergence]].
- **Multi-architecture procurement.** Pichai framed "custom TPUs, Axion CPUs, and the latest NVIDIA GPUs" as "the industry's widest variety of compute options," with Alphabet among the first to offer NVIDIA Vera Rubin — the multi-architecture pattern (Axion + Xeon + NVIDIA + TPU) documented at [[AI-agentic-CPU-orchestration-reemergence]].
- **[[AVGO]] reciprocal-confirmation deepens (A1 reciprocal-confirmation).** AVGO names Google as its lead XPU customer; GOOGL primary now confirms the TPU program *and its external-sales revenue* — AVGO builds, Alphabet designs and now sells. The external-TPU shift also sharpens Alphabet's competition with [[NVDA]] (selling TPUs into customers' own datacenters).
