# AMZN — Amazon.com, Inc.

## Thesis role

Amazon is the vault's **3rd hyperscaler page** (after [[MSFT]] + [[GOOGL]]) and **5th demand-side page**, the third ingest under the hyperscaler PAYER / SPENDER tracking program — the companies that *fund* the AI-datacenter buildout the rest of the vault tracks downstream (program scope: [[MSFT]] + GOOGL + AMZN + META + ORCL + CRWV). It is ingested for three purposes: (1) the **direct hyperscaler-CAPEX / AI-datacenter-expansion signal** (AWS) feeding [[AI-demand-durability]]; (2) as a **custom-silicon operator** (Trainium / Inferentia / Graviton) for [[hyperscaler-custom-ASIC]] and [[AI-agentic-CPU-orchestration-reemergence]]; and (3) as the **buyer-side counterparty** to the "Amazon NV Investment Holdings" supplier-warrant pattern the vault already documents from five supplier pages (see Vault touchpoint). Like the other hyperscalers — and unlike [[MSFT]]/[[NOW]]/[[PLTR]] — it is *not* on the [[software-AI-moat-durability]] application-layer scorecard.

**Placement framing (honest-verdict, Section 2.1).** A hyperscaler sits *above* Layer 1 in `frameworks.md` v10.1 — it is the **demand source** (Framework 3 dependency map) and the **CAPEX deployer** (Framework 10 allocation start), not a supply-side value-capture position. Amazon carries `layer: outside` and `outside` on all five per-domain tiers — it captures no supply-side chokepoint rent; it *funds* the chokepoints. Per the [[MSFT]]/[[GOOGL]] demand-side precedent.

**`frameworks.md` gap flag — RESOLVED by codification (S189).** The demand-side / hyperscaler / CAPEX-deployer placement was codified at CLAUDE.md §3.21 + frameworks.md Framework 10.1 (S189, v10.3): `layer: outside` + all `*_tier: outside` is a **documented value** ("not an AI-datacenter supply-chain value-capture participant"), theme-anchored not framework-placed. AMZN = a **capex-payer** sub-role. This Q2 refresh confirms the read; no placement change.

**Honest counterweights (Section 2.1):** (1) **free cash flow is negative in the build years** — TTM capex (~$173B) runs ahead of TTM operating cash flow (~$161B), and Amazon is **issuing debt** to fund AWS growth — the most FCF-pressured of the Big-4 hyperscalers (Jassy's "early-years FCF challenged" ROIC bet, now visible); (2) **two one-time expense benefits (~$1.2B) flattered Q2** — a ~$600M tariff refund (North America) + a ~$600M energy-derivative fair-value gain (AWS, ~130bps of the margin beat; clean AWS margin +520bps ex-that); (3) the **Anthropic entanglement is at extreme scale** — the $53.4B mark + a $20B financing facility + circular AWS-compute/Trainium commitments ([[AI-buildout-who-holds-the-risk]] Structure #3). **Placement HELD** (`layer: outside` + all `*_tier: outside`) — a strong AWS/capex quarter reinforces the demand-source role; the new "sell Trainium to third parties" consideration is a *future* possibility, not yet a business (§2.1 no-trigger).
