# META — Meta Platforms, Inc.

## Thesis role

Meta is the vault's **4th hyperscaler page** (after [[MSFT]] + [[GOOGL]] + [[AMZN]]) and **6th demand-side page** — the fourth ingest under the hyperscaler PAYER/SPENDER tracking program, and the one that completes the **four big hyperscalers** underlying the ~$640-720B 2026 AI-infrastructure aggregate the chokepoint thesis depends on. It is ingested for: (1) the **AI-datacenter capex / demand signal** feeding [[AI-demand-durability]]; (2) as a **custom-silicon operator** (MTIA, built with Broadcom) for [[hyperscaler-custom-ASIC]]; and (3) for its dense cross-vault footprint — [[AVGO]] (MTIA), [[AMD]] (MI450 co-developer), [[ARM]] (AGI-CPU lead partner), and [[GLW]] ($6B fiber). Like the other hyperscalers — and unlike [[MSFT]]/[[NOW]]/[[PLTR]] — it is *not* on the [[software-AI-moat-durability]] application-layer scorecard.

**Placement framing (honest-verdict, Section 2.1).** A hyperscaler sits *above* Layer 1 in `frameworks.md` v10.1 — the **demand source** (Framework 3) and **CAPEX deployer** (Framework 10), not a supply-side value-capture position. Meta carries `layer: outside` and `outside` on all five per-domain tiers — it captures no supply-side chokepoint rent; it *funds* the chokepoints. Per the [[MSFT]]/[[GOOGL]]/[[AMZN]] precedent.

**`frameworks.md` gap flag — RESOLVED by codification (S189).** The demand-side / hyperscaler / CAPEX-deployer placement was codified at CLAUDE.md §3.21 + frameworks.md Framework 10.1 (S189, v10.3): `layer: outside` + all `*_tier: outside` is a **documented value** ("not an AI-datacenter supply-chain value-capture participant"), theme-anchored not framework-placed. META = a **capex-payer** sub-role. This Q2 refresh confirms the read; no placement change.

### Q2 2026 refresh headline (S200, first true primary refresh) — down bottom line on one-timers, demand signal strengthened

**A DOWN-bottom-line quarter that is entirely one-time charges.** Q2 2026 revenue grew **+28% to $60.8B**, but **GAAP operating income FELL −8% YoY to $18.8B** (net income $15.85B/−14%; diluted EPS $6.18/−13%) — the decline is **fully explained by a $2.4B legal-proceedings charge + $1.2B severance** (the May 2026 ~8,000-person layoff). Ex those, operating income would have **risen +9% YoY** (CFO Susan Li). So the *operating* ads engine kept compounding (ad revenue +27%, price/ad +12%, GEM/Advantage+ gains), and the **AI demand signal strengthened** — the FY2026 capex floor was raised, 2027 plans are "geared towards maximizing 2026 and 2027 capacity," a **new BlackRock 1 GW data-center JV** was announced, and **"selling compute directly" was confirmed** ("offers at a significant premium over what we paid").

**Honest counterweights (Section 2.1) grew in the same quarter:** (1) **free cash flow collapsed to $784M** (Q2 capex $31.1B nearly consumed all operating cash; debt rose to $83.7B) — META joins [[GOOGL]]/[[MSFT]]/[[ORCL]] as a Big-4 payer whose FCF now runs at/below capex; (2) the build is **increasingly off-balance-sheet-financed** (the BlackRock JV + the non-consolidated Hyperion / Blue Owl VIE — the [[AI-buildout-who-holds-the-risk]] structure); (3) a **new third-party-AI-token cost** line appeared; (4) **ongoing youth-litigation material-loss risk** (the $2.4B charge + trials scheduled this year); (5) **Reality Labs still bleeds ~$(4.6)B/quarter**. **Placement HELD** (`layer: outside` + all `*_tier: outside`) — the compute-resale business shifts framing, not placement (selling compute ≠ owning a chokepoint; §2.1 no-trigger).

**A useful contrast to note (vs [[AI-earnings-quality-noncash-marks]]):** META's Q2 bottom-line decline is a **real, one-time operating charge** (legal + severance — cash costs you normalize for a clean-quarter view), **not a non-cash mark**.
